# Mitra Naeimi, PhD

**Geospatial data scientist and ecological modeller** · Remote sensing · Ensemble species-distribution modelling · Web-GIS

I build machine-learning models on satellite and field data and turn them into reproducible workflows and
interactive maps that decision makers can use. PhD in Remote Sensing and GIS (2025), with 20 years of
professional GIS work behind it: spatial databases, web-GIS platforms, team leadership and teaching.

### What I work on
- **Ensemble species-distribution / habitat-suitability models** in R with the `sdm` package
  (random forest, boosted regression trees, SVM, neural networks, AUC-weighted ensembles, TSS / AUC / kappa evaluation)
- **Satellite remote sensing of water**: MODIS chlorophyll-a, SST and reflectance; Sentinel-2 water indices (MNDWI)
- **Large-raster workflows**: tile-wise prediction, collinearity screening, uncertainty and variable importance
- **Web-GIS**: R Shiny, Leaflet, PostgreSQL/PostGIS on Linux servers
